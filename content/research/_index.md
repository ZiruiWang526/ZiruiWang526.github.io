      text: |-
        **Scroll down to the bottom of the page check out my projects!**

        Electrochemical research typically requires systematic exploration of these parameters for optimizing performance and uncovering meaningful structure–property–performance relationships. However, because most electrochemical characterization is traditionally performed using manual, low-throughput
        techniques, making comprehensive screening of complex systems is often labor-intensive and impractical. Moreover, electrochemical research continues to face reproducibility challenges arising from unstandardized protocols and human variability.

        In my doctoral research, I designed and integrated experimental systems that combine microfabricated electrodes with Python-controlled hardware, including potentiostats, custom electronics, and automated solution-handling robotics for high-throughput evaluation of battery materials. This platform addresses the limitations of traditional electrochemical workflows, significantly improving experimental throughput, robustness, and reproducibility. I have also connected the insights obtained from these automated, high-throughput experiments with practical battery performance by scaling laboratory discoveries toward device-level battery testing. Additionally, I deployed the platform to collaborators at other institutions, expanding its applications into broader electrochemical research and engineering problems.
