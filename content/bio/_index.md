### Short Bio
I was born and raised in [Shenyang, Liaoning, China](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shenyang), a city in northeastern China known for its strong heavy-industry heritage, which first sparked my interest in materials science and metallurgy. During my undergraduate studies, I developed a strong interest in semiconductors and integrated circuits and therefore pursued a minor in electrical engineering. Through this training, I gained experience in solid-state physics, automation, and microfabrication. Later, coursework in electrochemical energy storage systems introduced me to the field of electrochemistry and its potential impact on sustainable energy technologies, motivating a gradual shift in my research interests toward next-generation electrochemical energy storage systems. Currently, I enjoy my research in the [Rodríguez-López Laboratory](https://rodriguezlopez.chemistry.illinois.edu/), where my background in materials science, electrical engineering, and automation naturally converge. <br>

Outside the laboratory, I enjoy watching and playing soccer and am a passionate supporter of [Liverpool FC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liverpool_F.C.). I also enjoy playing and watching billiards (especially snooker), skiing, reading books, and listening to indie rock music. Also, I value time spent with my significant others—my girlfriend [Yuyuan](https://ziruiwang526.github.io/uploads/Yuyuan_Recital.JPG), who is pursuing a Doctor of Musical Arts (DMA) degree in flute performance at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, and my furry collaborator, Burger (汉堡🐈). <br>
