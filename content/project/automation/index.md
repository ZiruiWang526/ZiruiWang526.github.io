## Overview

This project focuses on developing automated electrochemical platforms that integrate
microfabricated interdigitated electrode arrays, custom PCBs, and Python-based control
software to enable reproducible, high-throughput experimentation.

## Key components
- Microfabricated electrodes
- Python-based automation
- Hardware integration: potentiostat, custom electronics, solution-handling robotics

## Impact
Designed and microfabricated interdigitated electrode arrays and developed a Python-controlled experimental automation
system integrating potentiostats, solution-handling robots, custom electronics, and Arduino microcontrollers, to overcome the
limitation of traditional electrochemical techniques for evaluating battery materials. <br>
Conducted process optimization and standardization, improving experimental reproducibility and robustness by ~20% while reducing manual
operations by ~80%. <br>
