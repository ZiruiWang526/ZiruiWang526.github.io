## Overview

This project involves applying automated platform for high-throughput electrochemical testing of electrodeposited redox-active
polymers (RAPs), including polypyrrole and a pyrene-4,5,9,10-tetrone azo group-based polymer polymer. The behavior of RAPs were evaluated
across diverse electrolyte systems, completing >500 experiments in <50 hours per material. The effect of electrolyte compositions on the
influences interfacial charge-transfer processes was confirmed by finite-element modeling and materials characterization techniques.

## Key components
- Electrodeposition
- Automated electrochemistry
- Finite-element modeling
- Materials characterization
