## Overview

This project aims to leveraging the capabilities of the automated electrochemistry platform to perform semi-close loop optimization of Prussian Blue-based
energy materials under diverse electrolyte compositions. Fully automated electrochemical characterizations of electrodeposited Prussian Blue were conducted, which revealed how electrolyte composition influences energy density, interfacial charge-transfer kinetics, and the chemical stability. These findings were supported by structural-property relationships obtained from materials characterization techniques such as X-ray diffraction (XRD), optical profilometry, and Raman spectroscopy. <br>
Currently, we are collaborating with the [Wang](https://www.pnnl.gov/people/wei-wang) and [Feng](https://www.pnnl.gov/people/ruozhu-feng) groups at Pacific northwest national laboratory (PNNL) to transfer the findings from automated, low-fidelity experiments to high-fidelity, device-level testings. The goal is to scale up laboratory discoveries by connecting experimental insights with practical battery performance, revealing how automated experimentation and data-driven analysis can support engineering decisions and the scale-up of next-generation battery technologies.
