## Overview

Deployed the platform to collaborators at other institutions, including the [Patel group](https://pme.uchicago.edu/group/patel-group) at the University of Chicago,
[Cabana group](https://cabana.chem.uic.edu/) at Argonne national laboratory/University of Illinois Chicago, the [Feng](https://www.pnnl.gov/people/ruozhu-feng) group at
Pacific northwest national laboratory, expanding its applications into broader electrochemical research and engineering problems such as energy storage, sensing, water
purification, and electro-catalysis.

## Key components
- Automated electrochemistry
- Process development
